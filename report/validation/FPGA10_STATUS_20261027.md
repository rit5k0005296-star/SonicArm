## Open sign-off items

- The inherited V8 Clock Wizard creates two timing-methodology critical warnings (`TIMING-4`, `TIMING-27`).
- The official PYNQ `rgb2dvi` generated-clock XDC creates `TIMING-36` because the dynamic pixel and 5x serial clocks are separate outputs of a run-time reconfigurable MMCM. The bitstream is generated and timed, but this warning must be resolved or explicitly justified before calling the design timing-clean.
- External audio/HDMI ports have no board-level input/output delay model. There are no unconstrained internal endpoints.
- Board `192.168.2.99` loaded the third-release matched BIT/HWH pair. The
  synchronous-direct smoke test displayed the expected 1280x720 color bars on
  the physical HDMI OUT screen. The USB touch controller enumerated as
  `222a:0001 OpenWare Multi-Touch-V5000` on `/dev/input/event0`; its absolute
  range is X 0..800 and Y 0..480 with normal orientation, and five-point touch
  events were observed. Rendered application UI, microphone/voiceprint
  coexistence, and end-to-end operation remain pending.

## Important display boundary

PYNQ-Z2 HDMI OUT in this design is a PL VDMA video stream, not a Linux desktop/DRM display. A browser opened on the board will not automatically appear on HDMI OUT. The included `hdmi_smoketest.py` first validates the physical PL display path with color bars. After that, the UI requires a PS renderer/frame-pump (or a DRM/KMS integration) to copy rendered UI frames into the VDMA buffers; USB touch remains a Linux HID input path.
